## Plan (agent output, reviewed by the human before implementation)

The human proposed `Solid/Curve.rowFill(dir:Vector|Axis|MinusAxis, distance:number|Shape)`. The
agent's review, as accepted with the human's changes:

- Add the spacing, centre to centre, and put the direction after it, like `row(count, spacing, direction = 'x')`.
- `spacing` means centre to centre here and the gap between copies in `row()`: a note in the TSDoc.
- `until` is a length from the back face of the shape to the far side of the last member, or a
  shape to fill up to (its near bbox face); throw when it lies behind.
- `distribute` as the 4th argument: `'grid'` (default, members on the grid from the start, a
  closing member flush at the end, as `make.wall` does) or `'even'` (equal bays of at most the spacing).
- `close` as the last argument: `false` leaves the end member out.
- Directions as `PointLike|Axis`, with minus axes; return a `ShapeCollection` with the original
  first, named with `ShapeCollection._nameRow`, as `row()`.
- The placement in one helper, so `make.wall` and the brep kernel can share it later.

Final signature: `rowFill(until, spacing, direction = 'x', distribute = 'grid', close = true)` on
meshup `Mesh` and `Curve`. The minus axes became a new `AxisDirection` type instead of widening
`Axis`, which ~100 places use as `'x'|'y'|'z'` (cutoff(), setComponent(), ...).

Then the brep version on request: `Shape.rowFill()` in `packages/core/src/modeler/brep/Shape.ts`
on the same `rowFillOffsets()`, with a mesh/brep parity test in `shape-parity.test.ts`.

## Review and decisions by the human
- Argument order: `distribute` 4th, `close` last; spacing difference with `row()` only noted in TSDoc.
- Asked for the brep version too.
